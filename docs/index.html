02 — Attention-aware delivery

It doesn’t just look quiet.
It waits for you.

Beyond how a cue looks, Periphery decides when it deserves your eyes. Every cue passes through the same gauntlet: focus hold → slack tide → immediate. Only Tier 1 cues and meeting reminders go straight through.

Slack Tide

While you’re mid-keystroke-burst, non-urgent cues are held and released in your next natural micro-pause — about six seconds without input. Pills never pile up, nothing is held longer than 90 seconds, and a flood collapses into a single +N more. No keylogging: just the OS idle timer.

you, mid-flow

 

The Constellation

Cues held during focus don’t vanish into a counter. Each leaves a dim, twinkling star near the corner of your screen. A glance tells you how much happened — without telling you what, which is exactly the information that breaks flow. When focus ends, the stars dissolve into one quiet summary.

5 updates — gitlab 3 · outlook 2

Focus Assist, actually respected

While Windows would suppress toasts — Focus Assist, presentation mode, a full-screen app — Periphery holds everything but Tier 1 and meeting reminders, then flushes one quiet summary glow when focus ends. Your DND setting finally means what it says.

03 — Privacy & security

Private by architecture.
Not by policy.

Periphery was built for strict enterprise environments where data exfiltration and cloud dependencies are unacceptable. There is no privacy setting to trust — there is simply nowhere for your data to go.

No cloud backend

No central server. No telemetry. The only outbound requests are the ones you configure — straight to GitLab and Microsoft Graph, from your machine. The overlay never loads a single byte from the network.

Encrypted secret storage

Tokens are encrypted with your OS credential store and only ciphertext touches disk. No preload bridge can read a secret back out.

safeStorage → DPAPI · Keychain · libsecret

Loopback-only webhook

The local cue receiver binds to loopback and is unreachable from the network, with allowlist validation on every untrusted payload.

bind 127.0.0.1:49123 · cuePayload allowlist

No third-party brokers

Integrations run locally, so there’s no Zapier, no Make.com, no automation middleman holding your credentials on the wrong side of the corporate firewall.

Renderers run sandboxed · context isolation · restrictive CSP · narrow contextBridge APIs

04 — The Connector Engine

Wired to your tools.
Not to their clouds.

A modular ConnectorManager polls APIs directly from your machine. Connectors receive credentials through an injected secret store — they never import Electron, which keeps them unit-tested, portable, and honest.
